1. Bring a large pot of water with the coarse salt to the boil.
2. Toast the pistachios in a dry frying pan for 3 minutes until fragrant. Let them cool.
3. Blend the nuts, basil, garlic, lemon zest, lemon juice and Parmesan with the stick blender. Pour in the olive oil while blending until you have a smooth pesto. Season with salt and pepper.
4. Cook the spaghetti al dente according to the packet. Save a cup of the cooking water.
5. Toss the pasta with the pesto and a splash of cooking water until glossy. Serve straight away.
